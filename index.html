Supporting Afghan Women

What's Happening?

Afghan women and girls are experiencing increasing restrictions on their rights, education, and freedom of expression. Many feel isolated and unheard, with limited access to the outside world.

Why We Created This?

We created this platform to build meaningful, supportive connections between people around the world and Afghan women. Through letters and messages, we aim to remind them they are not alone and that their voices matter.
