# afghan-women
This website allows users to explore information about the challenges Afghan women face, access helpful resources, and learn how to support them. The site is built to foster empathy, awareness, and action as Afghan women experience restrictions on their rights, education, and freedom of expression.

Users can also sign up to become a pen pal by filling out a form, helping people connect.

## Pages
### Home Page: 
Gives an overview of the challenges that Afghan women are facing, as well as our motivation for creating this website. 
### Information Page:
Includes in-depth information on the restriction of Afghan women's rights, health, and education. Also includes personal stories from Afghan women themselves.
### Resources Page:
Includes links to organizations that are committed to helping Afghan women.
### Pen Pal Page:
Includes a form where Afghan women are paired with people from around the world, so they can connect and feel less alone.
### Citations Page:
Includes APA citations for the information included.
